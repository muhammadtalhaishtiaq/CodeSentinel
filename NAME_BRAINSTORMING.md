- **Too long** (3 syllables = hard to remember)

- **Hard to pronounce** (Sen-ti-nel? Sen-tri-nel?)
- **.io domain might be taken** (sentinelsecurity.io probably exists)
- **No brand personality** (boring enterprise name)

## 🏆 TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS

### **#1: BLAZE** 🔥
**Domain:** blaze.dev or blazeai.dev
**Tagline:** "Blazing fast security scanning for developers"
**Vibe:** Aggressive, fast, memorable
**Why Win:**
- ✅ One syllable (easy to remember)
- ✅ Strong branding (fire = speed + power)
- ✅ Tech-friendly (sounds like a startup)
- ✅ Easy to say ("Check Blaze" vs "Check CodeSentinel")
- ✅ Perfect for marketing: "Let it blaze" (features)
- ✅ Good domain availability
- **Similar companies:** BlazeMeter (performance testing), Blaze AI (data)

### **#2: PULSE** 💓
**Domain:** pulse.dev or pulsesecurity.dev
**Tagline:** "Real-time security at the heartbeat of your code"
**Vibe:** Alive, real-time, connected
**Why Win:**
- ✅ Signals real-time scanning (5 seconds!)
- ✅ Medical/heartbeat metaphor = trust + health
- ✅ One syllable (memorable)
- ✅ Marketing: "Keep the pulse on your security"
- ✅ Analytics angle: Dashboard shows "security pulse"
- **Similar:** Datadog uses "pulse" terminology

### **#3: FORGE** 🔨
**Domain:** forge.dev or forgeai.dev
**Tagline:** "Forge secure code at every build"
**Vibe:** Build/create, strong, craftmanship
**Why Win:**
- ✅ Implies building/creating things securely
- ✅ Blacksmith forge = strength, craftsmanship
- ✅ GitHub Forge is coming (opportunity to differentiate)
- ✅ "Forge better code" (marketing angle)
- **Con:** Might confuse with other "Forge" products

---

## 🎯 FINAL RECOMMENDATION: **BLAZE**

**Why it's the best:**

1. **Brand Power** - Sounds like a fast, aggressive startup
2. **SEO Friendly** - "Blaze security scanner", "Blaze vs Snyk"
3. **Marketing Magic** - Lots of anagrams:
   - "Let it Blaze" (features)
   - "Blaze through scans" (speed)
   - "Set your security ablaze" (power)
   - "No-fuss blazing security" (simplicity)
4. **International** - Works in multiple languages (Latin root)
5. **Domain** - blazesecurity.dev or blaze.dev (check availability)
6. **Pronunciation** -eway English speakers say it correctly
7. **Competitor Positioning** - Snyk is slow and corporate, Blaze is fast and exciting
